How this website uses information you choose to share.

Your bag

The bag stores product identifiers and quantities in your browser’s local storage. It does not store your name, email, phone number or message. Empty the bag or clear this site’s browser data to remove it.

Enquiries and messages

Preparing a summary keeps your details in the current page. They are only submitted when you choose an available sending option. An email draft opens your email application for you to review and send. Downloaded summaries are stored on your own device.

If website email delivery is available, the bakery uses your name, contact details and message to respond to the request. An email provider processes that delivery. Please do not include payment-card details or sensitive personal information.

Maps

The Google map loads only when you ask it to. It connects to Google, which may process connection information under its own privacy policy. The site does not ask for your device location. You can unload the map at any time.

Preferences and analytics

Your motion preference may be saved in your browser. This version does not include advertising trackers, analytics or a newsletter subscription.

Questions or removal requests

Use the contact page to ask about your information. Do not send personal information until the bakery’s contact and delivery details have been confirmed.
