Our Story

Umhlanga Bakehouse was founded with love for fresh, daily baking. We use only premium ingredients to bring you bread, cakes and pastries that taste like home. Located in the heart of Umhlanga, we bake every morning at 4am.

Our mission is to provide affordable luxury - great taste at great prices, for every family.
