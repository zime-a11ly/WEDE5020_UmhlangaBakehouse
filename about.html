Umhlanga Bakehouse began with a love for fresh, daily baking. Our family-owned bakery brings bread, cakes and pastries to the community around us.

Each morning starts at 4am, with time to prepare, bake and put care into the little details. From an everyday loaf to a cake for a special occasion, we want every bite to feel worth slowing down for.

Our mission is simple: affordable luxury, great taste and something lovely for every family.

Can I order a custom cake?

Yes. Share your occasion, preferred date and ideas on our enquiry page. We’ll confirm the design, price and availability before arranging the order.

How does the online bag work?

Add your favourites and adjust quantities in your bag. Then prepare an enquiry with your selection. Payment and collection are arranged after the bakery confirms your request.

Do you cater for allergies?

Please include any allergies or dietary requirements in your enquiry. Our bakes may contain wheat, milk, eggs and nuts, and we cannot promise an allergen-free kitchen.

When are you open?

Our listed hours are 06:00–18:00 daily. Please confirm your collection arrangements and the exact address before travelling.
